Team building in Barcelona: magic and workshops for teams

October 2026 · 4 min read

Ideas for an original team activity in Barcelona: magic, a hands-on workshop, or both, and how to choose based on your group.

A different team activity

Dinners, paintball, a treasure hunt. They all have their place, but they start to feel the same. If you are looking for something different for your team in Barcelona, two formats work very well and can be combined: a magic show and a hands-on workshop.

Magic for teams: shared laughter

A magician at a company lunch or at the close of a day gets people who do not see each other every day laughing together for a good while. Close-up magic works well during the meal, and a short act is a great way to close an event.

The audience comes first: the magic has to work for everyone, without putting anyone on the spot. A good magician adapts it to the group.

Workshops: working together without noticing

A hands-on workshop calls for coordination, sharing tasks and talking as a whole group. At Lannex Lab, perfume and cosmetics workshops for teams, each person creates their own perfume and the group finishes with something to take home. It is a very useful format when the goal is to get to know each other better.

How to choose: if the goal is to laugh and switch off, magic is the most direct option. If the goal is for the team to work together and get to know each other, a workshop fits better. And if you want both, a workshop at the start and magic to close the meal is a very common combination.
