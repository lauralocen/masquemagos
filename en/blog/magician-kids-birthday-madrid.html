Kids' birthdays

Magician for a kids' birthday in Madrid: at home, in a venue or outdoors

November 2026 · 6 min read

In Madrid a children's birthday party almost always happens in one of three places: the living room at home, a venue rented for two hours, or the garden of the housing complex. A magician works in all three, but not in the same way, and the difference is decided before you book, not on the day.

The three places a birthday party happens in Madrid

What shapes the party is not the number of children. It is the square metres.

  • At home. A Madrid flat, with the living room cleared and the sofas pushed against the wall. The most common setting, and the one that raises the most doubts.
  • At a party venue. Two hours rented, rubber flooring, paper tablecloths and sometimes an entertainer included.
  • Outdoors. The garden of the housing complex, a communal area, a house with a plot or a park.

None of the three is a problem. What changes is where the children sit and whether a sound system is needed.

At home: the living room holds more than you think

The question we are asked most is whether it fits in a flat. Almost always, it does.

A magic show needs two things: the magician's back covered, meaning a wall behind him, and room for the children to sit on the floor in front. With the sofa moved and the dining table pushed to one side, a normal Madrid living room holds fifteen seated children without trouble.

No stage, no platform, no power socket for lights. If the flat is small the sound is turned down or simply not used: at three metres the voice carries.

What is worth deciding in advance is where the adults go. Parents standing behind the children is fine. Parents mixed in among them is not: the children turn round to find their mother every two minutes and the show falls apart.

At a party venue: check what the package covers

Venues in Madrid are usually rented by the two-hour slot, and many include an entertainer in the price.

That is not the same thing as a magic show, and it is worth knowing before paying twice for the same thing. An entertainer keeps the party going with games throughout; a magician performs a one-hour show with a beginning and an end, and the children sit down for it.

If the venue already has an entertainer, the best combination is games at the start, the show in the middle and cake at the end. What does not work is magic in the last fifteen minutes, once the cake has been eaten.

Outdoors: shade, ground and sound

In Madrid a patio or garden is the best option from April to June and from September to October. In July and August, at midday, it is not.

Three things to check first:

  • Shade for the audience. Children sitting in the sun for twenty minutes get up on their own.
  • Firm ground. Loose sand and gravel make the show harder.
  • Sound. Outdoors it is always needed. We bring it, but we need a socket, or advance notice to bring a battery.

If it is a public park, it is worth checking with the district council whether a permit is needed to set up sound. In a communal area, telling the residents' association is enough.

What age it works from

We do children's birthdays from age 5.

Below that a one-hour show does not hold up: it is not that they dislike magic, it is that they cannot stay seated and the format stops working for everyone. At five they follow a story, they understand that something is impossible, and they remember it the next day.

If the party mixes ages, from five to eleven, the show is built with that in mind. The little ones take the visual part and the older ones the impossible part.

One hour, and when to place it

The show lasts one hour. Always, in all three settings.

The right moment is before the cake, never after. Once the cake comes out the party is emotionally over: the children get up, the parents start collecting coats and nobody is sitting down again.

For a party running from four to seven in the afternoon, magic from five to six and cake at six works best.

The parents end up watching

This is the part nobody expects when they book it.

A children's party is usually an hour and a half of parents standing in the kitchen checking the time. When there is a show, they stand behind the children and end up watching just as closely, because magic done properly is not only for kids.

Half the parties we do come from exactly that: a mother who saw the show at another child's birthday.

Frequently asked questions

What age do you do children's birthdays from?

From age 5. Below that a one-hour show does not hold up, because the children cannot stay seated and the format stops working.

How much space is needed in a flat?

A normal living room with the sofa moved aside. The magician needs a wall behind him and room for the children to sit on the floor in front. No stage or platform is needed.

How many children does it take?

There is no minimum or maximum. Eight works and twenty-five works. Above twenty-five it is worth telling us, so the space can be set up differently.

What if the venue already includes an entertainer?

They combine: games at the start, the magic show in the middle of the party and cake at the end. What is best avoided is magic after the cake.

Which areas of Madrid do you cover?

Madrid city and the whole Madrid region. Travel within the city is included in the price; if the party is outside it, we give you the full closed figure before you book.

How far in advance should I book?

Saturday afternoons go first, especially from February to June. Two or three weeks ahead is usually enough, but in high season it is worth asking as soon as you have the date.
