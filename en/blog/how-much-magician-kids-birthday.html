What makes the price vary

1. The date and season

Weekends and high-demand seasons (Christmas, summer, school holidays) have less availability, which can affect price. Booking ahead gives you more room on both price and availability.

2. The magician's experience and track record

A magician with years of real experience in children's parties and good references tends to charge a bit more than someone just starting out — precisely because that experience is what guarantees the party will go well.

At Más que Magos, sound equipment is always included in the price. You don't need to worry about anything or hire any extras: we arrive with everything needed for the show to run smoothly from start to finish.

How to know if the price is fair

Rather than just looking at the number, check these before booking:

  • Do they have real experience with kids' birthday parties, or only adult events?
  • Can you see a video of a real performance, with real children?
  • Does the price include all the material, or is something charged separately?
  • Do they ask the children's ages before proposing anything?

A good magician for a kids' birthday party is not the one who charges most, but the one with real experience performing for children who gives you the closed price in writing before the day. That is what to look at.

What if the party has kids of very different ages?

A good professional magician knows how to adapt to the children's ages and the group in front of them. It doesn't matter if there are younger or older kids at the same party: they'll know how to engage everyone equally, adapting to each group as they read the room. A bad magician is exactly the one who can't do this, and always repeats the same show no matter who's watching.
