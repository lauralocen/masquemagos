Magician for a corporate Christmas dinner: price, format and when to perform

November 2026 · 4 min read

What people search for before booking a magician for the Christmas dinner: how much it costs, which format works and when in the evening to perform.

How much does a magician cost for a corporate Christmas dinner?

The price depends on the format and the duration. To give you an exact figure we need the date, how many people you are and what the room is like. With those three details we reply within 24 hours, with no obligation.

Close-up magic or a show on stage?

It depends on the room and the moment. Close-up magic works table by table while people eat and chat. A stage show needs everyone to look at the same place, so it fits better at the end of the dinner.

If you have many guests, the most common option is to combine both: close-up magic during dinner and a short act to close the after-dinner.

When in the dinner should the magician perform?

  • Between courses: ideal to liven up the table when the energy drops.
  • During the after-dinner: the longest moment, with the whole group together.
  • At the close: a short closing act that people remember.

Can I book a magician for a dinner of 40 or 100 people?

Yes. With forty people, a magician can visit every table in one or two rounds. With a hundred, it is usual to schedule two or three passes by area, or an act on stage for the whole room.

What should the magician know about the room?

The key thing is that the audience can see. Pillars, long narrow rooms and an open bar change the format a lot. If you send us a photo of the empty room, we can see it straight away.

Tip: the magic has to work for the whole group, without putting anyone on the spot. A good magician adapts it to the company and to the average age of the team.

Tell us the date, number of guests and type of venue, and we'll suggest the best format.
