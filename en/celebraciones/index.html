Frequently asked questions

How much does a magician cost for a celebration?

Prices are fixed: children's birthday 195€, adult birthday 225€, christenings 295€, communions 295€, weddings 315€ (close-up magic) or 400€ (ceremony master with magic), and hen/stag parties 225€.

How long do you take to reply?

We confirm availability for your date within 24 hours. The fastest way is to message us on WhatsApp.

How long does the show last?

1 hour. If you want more time, we tell you the price before booking.

Where do you perform?

We work across Catalonia and the Community of Madrid, including Barcelona and Madrid. Travel within the city is included in the price.

Do you have entertainers as well as magicians?

Yes. For children's birthdays and for the children at communions and weddings we have entertainment with games and music, on its own or together with the magic show.

How far in advance should I book?

The sooner the better, especially for weekends and spring communions. Even so, ask us, even if it is for a few days from now.

What does the magician need to perform?

Nothing special: the magician brings all their equipment. If anything is needed (a specific space, sound…), we tell you before booking.
