Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

How much does a magician for a communion cost?

We have options for different budgets. Tell us about your celebration and we'll recommend the best option, no obligation.

How long does a magic performance for a celebration last?

Performances last around 1 hour, although they can be adapted to any type of celebration depending on what you need.

Do the magicians travel anywhere in Spain?

Yes, we have professional magicians across Spain for communions, christenings, birthdays, weddings and bachelor/ette parties.

How far in advance should I book the magician?

We recommend booking at least 1 week in advance, though we also try to accommodate last-minute requests depending on availability.

What does the magician need to perform at my celebration?

The magician brings all the equipment needed for the show. If your celebration requires anything special (a specific space, sound system, etc.) we'll discuss it with you, and any extra cost would be your responsibility.
