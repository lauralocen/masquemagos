Magician for corporate events in Madrid: formats, venues and how to book

November 2026 · 7 min read

Madrid is one of the busiest cities in Spain for corporate events: conventions, team dinners, product launches, trade fairs at IFEMA and end-of-year celebrations. In that context, making your event memorable is not easy. A well-chosen magician is one of the simplest ways to do it. In this guide we explain which formats work, in which venues, and how to book without surprises.

Why hire a magician for a corporate event

At a corporate event, filling the agenda is not the hard part: creating moments people talk about the next day is. Magic does that for several reasons:

  • It breaks the ice. At a drinks reception where not everyone knows each other, a magician approaching a group sparks conversation instantly.
  • It works with any audience. Executives, sales teams, international clients: magic needs no language or context.
  • It can be personalised. Your company logo appearing on a card, a message from management revealed at the end, your product appearing out of nowhere.
  • It doesn't interrupt. Close-up magic blends into the event without stopping anything.

Which format to choose

Close-up magic

The magician moves among guests, group by group, with cards, coins and personal objects. It is the star format for receptions, cocktails and standing dinners, and works as well with 20 people as with 200.

Stage show

A one-hour show for the whole audience at once. Ideal as the centrepiece of a gala dinner, a convention or an end-of-year party, with audience participation and, if you like, company messages built in.

Mentalism

Mind reading, predictions, impossible coincidences. It works especially well with adult, professional audiences and at events that call for a more elegant tone.

Magic on a trade fair stand

At fairs such as those held at IFEMA, a magician on your stand attracts visitors, keeps them there and makes it easier for your sales team to start the conversation. Your product can be built into the effects.

Typical venues in Madrid and how they affect the choice

  • Hotels and event venues: ready for anything; a stage show fits without problems.
  • Restaurants with a private room: table-to-table close-up magic or a short show between courses works best.
  • Unique venues and estates outside the city: plan for travel time and set-up schedules.
  • Your own offices: great for internal celebrations; all you need is a clear area.
  • Exhibition centres: close-up magic on the stand, with shifts matched to peak visitor times.

When to book

Madrid has high-demand seasons: November and December for Christmas dinners, and spring for conventions and trade fairs. For those dates, book your magician several weeks ahead. Off-season there is more flexibility, but the earlier you confirm, the easier it is to tailor the format to your event.

What to ask before you book

  • Which format do they recommend for my event? A good supplier asks about your audience, venue and goals before proposing anything.
  • What does the price include? Travel, sound if needed, performance time.
  • Can it be personalised? Logo, product, a message from management.
  • Do they provide an invoice? Essential for a company event.

How to fit it into the event programme

  • At the welcome reception: close-up magic to break the ice while people arrive.
  • Between courses: when dinner starts to drag, a magic set is perfect.
  • Before speeches or awards: a show that gathers the room's attention.
  • As the closing act: a finale with a company message leaves everyone on a high.

Ideas to personalise the magic at your event

  • Your logo at the key moment. A signed card that reappears with the company logo, or the logo revealed at the end of the show.
  • A message from management. A prediction that matches the year's motto or the convention's goal.
  • Your product as the star. At a launch, the new product appears out of nowhere on stage.
  • The year's figures. A sales figure or company milestone that "the magician guesses".
  • A tribute. At a retirement or company anniversary, a routine dedicated to the person being honoured.

To personalise it, we just need you to share the idea a few days ahead and, if needed, the logo or product.

Common mistakes when booking corporate magic

  • Choosing the format without thinking about the space. A stage show in a restaurant with no clear area won't shine.
  • Scheduling the magic when people are leaving. The best moment is when everyone is still in the room.
  • Not telling other suppliers. Coordinating with the caterers and the sound technician avoids waiting around.
